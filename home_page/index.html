Lattice density-functional theory in Lean

by Miguel A. L. Marques

A Lean 4 / Mathlib formalization of the foundations of DFT for finite lattice systems: ground states, the Hohenberg–Kohn theorem and its failures, second-quantized lattice fermions, a generic Hohenberg–Kohn theorem, and Kohn–Sham theory.
